## Which options are confirmed in the shipped game, on which platform, by which source and date?

### Takeaway
A consistent core set appears across several independent launch-week sources, most of them written from the PS5 build:
- an "Accessibility" group under Settings > Gameplay
- Accessible Combat, Reduced Vehicle Damage, Auto Drive and Simplified Mini Games
- Tap Assist with 5 levels, Hold Assist / hold-to-toggle and hold delay
- aim assist
- resizable subtitles with a background and speaker names
- UI text size up to X Large
- colourblind modes, High Contrast Shaders and brightness reduction
- menu/screen narration and a separate audio description toggle
- a minimap On/Dynamic/Off setting plus size settings, and a heavily customisable HUD (about 70 UI options)

The most-repeated counts are Notebookcheck's: 25 gameplay accessibility, 12 display, 70 interface, 24 camera and 14 audio options. There is no official Rockstar feature list. Most detail traces back to reviews, streams and PS5 menus. Xbox-specific confirmation is thin and comes mainly from the Xbox Store listing.

### Cited Findings

- Path: Pause > Settings > Gameplay tab, with an "Accessibility" group near the bottom. Tap assist, hold assist and hold delay sit in that group. Other options are spread across the UI, Display, Camera, Audio and Controller tabs. Source describes the PS5 menus shortly after the Nov 19 launch — [allthings.how, "GTA 6 Accessibility Options on PS5: Where Each Setting Lives"](https://allthings.how/gta-6-accessibility-options/); [Vice Manual](https://vicemanual.com/gta-6-accessibility)

Vice Manual](https://vicemanual.com/gta-6-accessibility); [Dork](https://readdork.com/features/does-gta-6-easy-mode-no-but)

### Inferences

- The counts (25/12/70/24/14) appear to come from one original source, probably Rockstar or Eurogamer material or a single menu count, that both Notebookcheck and Eurogamer/Dork use. They are not independent confirmations. Vice Manual's separate "70 UI options" PS5 count does add some independent support for the 70 figure.
- For an Adel video, the safest items to show are the cross-confirmed core set: Accessible Combat, Reduced Vehicle Damage, Auto Drive, Simplified Mini Games, 5-level Tap Assist, Hold Assist, subtitles size/background/speaker name, UI text X Large, High Contrast, colourblind modes, narration plus audio description, and minimap On/Dynamic/Off. ## What did Rockstar say about accessibility being built in from the start (who said it, where, when)?

### Takeaway
Rockstar's Associate UI Director **Sam Grosset** said accessibility was "considered from the outset". This comes from Rockstar-provided material and interviews relayed by Creative Bloq and by Eurogamer (cited by Dork and Nuclear Coffee) around launch. Separately, **Ben Baylis**, Rockstar's senior production coordinator for accessibility, called GTA VI Rockstar's most accessible game, in The Outer Haven's review (Nov 18, 2026). Rockstar published no standalone accessibility announcement.

### Cited Findings
- Sam Grosset, Associate UI Director at Rockstar, said accessibility was "considered from the outset" and that "designing for diverse needs enables us to create better games for everyone". He gave three examples: a larger font size, a more minimal screen, and a tweak to the sprint control. The quote comes from a Creative Bloq piece on the GTA 6 UI/HUD (Pricedown font, in-hand phone) — [Creative Bloq, "How GTA 6's interface kept the classic Pricedown font but still managed to feel modern"](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/how-gta-6s-interface-kept-the-classic-pricedown-font-but-still-managed-to-feel-modern)
- The same Grosset quote is attributed to "materials Rockstar provided to Eurogamer before launch". The team wants players to have options "to play the game their way". Grosset cited "a larger font size, a more minimal screen experience, or a controls tweak to sprinting" — [Dork](https://readdork.com/features/does-gta-6-easy-mode-no-but); [Nuclear Coffee](https://nuclearcoffee.org/does-gta-6-have-an-easy-mode-not-officially-but-there-are-two-very-important-settings-you-can-change-to-make-the-game-less-difficult/)
- Ben Baylis, Rockstar's senior production coordinator for accessibility, said GTA VI is the most accessible game Rockstar has made. He cited text size adjustments and closed captions for non-speech sounds. Double Tap says this was Rockstar's only on-the-record accessibility comment, given through The Outer Haven's review on Nov 18, 2026 — [Double Tap](https://doubletaponair.com/gta-vi-brings-audio-description-and-narration-but-totally-blind-players-will-not-be-able-to-play-it-alone/); [The Outer Haven review](https://www.theouterhaven.net/grand-theft-auto-vi-review/)
- Rockstar confirmed menu narration, audio description for cinematics, high-contrast shaders and input assists, according to Double Tap — [Double Tap](https://doubletaponair.com/gta-vi-brings-audio-description-and-narration-but-totally-blind-players-will-not-be-able-to-play-it-alone/)
- Victor Dima is a blind accessibility consultant who, per Double Tap, has worked with Rockstar. He said he would not call the game fully accessible for blind players, but that it is "much more accessible than he expected" — [Double Tap](https://doubletaponair.com/gta-vi-brings-audio-description-and-narration-but-totally-blind-players-will-not-be-able-to-play-it-alone/); [Double Tap interview with Victor Dima](https://doubletaponair.com/victor-dima-on-gta-6-accessibility-ai-game-mods-and-the-game-awards-2/)

### Inferences
- The "from the start" message is Rockstar press messaging delivered through selected outlets (Eurogamer, Creative Bloq, The Outer Haven), not an official Newswire post. A video should attribute the quote to "Sam Grosset, Associate UI Director, Rockstar Games (via Eurogamer / Creative Bloq)".
- The Creative Bloq and Eurogamer quotes are probably from the same Rockstar press package, given the identical wording.

### Gaps
- The exact publication dates of the Creative Bloq and Eurogamer pieces were not visible. They appear to be launch-window (around Nov 18–20, 2026). The original Eurogamer article did not surface in search.
- The exact wording of Ben Baylis's quote was only seen paraphrased.
- There is no primary Rockstar source (Newswire, support site) for either statement.

## What is praised and what is missing or criticised by accessibility reviewers?

### Takeaway
Praise:
- the breadth of options ("impressive", "one of Rockstar's most customisable", more than any prior Rockstar game at launch)
- the "easy mode" toggles
- 5-level tap assist
- the blind/low-vision features (narration, audio description, high contrast), which Can I Play That calls "perhaps the most impressive"

Criticism:
- Rockstar did not give review copies to accessibility outlets or disabled creators, and published no pre-launch accessibility information
- there is no confirmed full in-game button remapping
- there are no difficulty levels
- the options are hard to find
- the game is not independently playable by totally blind players

## Are any reported numbers contradictory between sources?

### Takeaway
The headline counts are consistent: 25 gameplay (= 2 + 23), 12 display, 70 UI, 24 camera, 14 audio. The contradictions are in menu details:
- subtitle and UI text size steps
- whether narration is on or offered by default
- the Xbox Store "difficulty levels" tag against "no difficulty levels"
- the Xbox Store accessibility count, which grew from 5 to 13
- the release date (Nov 19 vs. 11/20 on the Xbox Store)

### Cited Findings
- 25 gameplay accessibility options (Notebookcheck) vs. "two toggles + 23 other" (Eurogamer via Dork). These are consistent (2 + 23 = 25) — [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-offsets-cheat-code-restrictions-with-more-easy-modes.1454752.0.html); [Dork](https://readdork.com/features/does-gta-6-easy-mode-no-but)
- UI options: 70 (Notebookcheck, Eurogamer/Dork, and Vice Manual's own PS5 count). Consistent — [Vice Manual](https://vicemanual.com/gta-6-accessibility)
- **Text and subtitle sizes conflict.**
  - One guide: UI Text Size Small/Medium/Large/X Large (4 steps); subtitles Off (default)/Small/Medium/Large.
  - Another: three subtitle sizes.
  - A third: "the UI has three text sizes".
  - "X Large" appears to apply to UI text, not to subtitles.

  — [Vice Manual](https://vicemanual.com/gta-6-accessibility); [gta6wiki.eu](https://www.gta6wiki.eu/guides/gta-6-accessibility/); [gta6map.net](https://www.gta6map.net/wiki/gta-6/guides/gta-6-accessibility-options)
- **Subtitle default conflicts.** Vice Manual's comparison table lists subtitles as "Mission Only" by default. Another guide says Off by default — [Vice Manual](https://vicemanual.com/gta-6-accessibility); [gta6wiki.eu](https://www.gta6wiki.eu/guides/gta-6-accessibility/)
- **Narration default conflicts.** Can I Play That says the screen reader is offered and available from first launch. Another guide says "Screen Narration" is Off by default. These may both be true (offered at setup, off unless chosen) — [Can I Play That](https://caniplaythat.com/2026/11/18/early-looks-at-gta-vi-accessibility-features/); [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/gta-6-accessibility-options/)
