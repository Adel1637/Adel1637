DESCRIPTION (EN)
Your colors and fonts are fighting. 5 basics to fix it:
1. One accent color, only for actions (buttons, links).
2. Neutrals for the rest: white and grays.
3. Two fonts max — one family is often enough.
4. A type scale: few sizes, clear steps (48, 24, 16).
5. Enough contrast: gray on white must stay readable (WCAG AA).
The interface in this video is fictional.
Follow for more UI/UX tutorials.
